# Knoll Mini — Design Language (refinement sessions)

Production-ready look, Microduck proportions, BDX-derived panel detailing.
Function is fixed: joint anchors, axes, ranges, servo positions, horn faces,
bearing seats and mounting interfaces never move. Only the skin changes.

## Surfaces
- Every outer surface is a designed surface: extruded/lofted 2D profiles with
  real fillets, or boxes with chamfers/fillets. No raster contours, no
  triangle-soup wrinkles, no sliver faces.
- Outer edge fillet family: 1.5 mm on big parts, 0.8 mm on small parts.
  One consistent fillet/chamfer language across the robot.
- Flat faces stay flat; curves are single-radius or gentle lofts.

## Walls and structure
- Shell wall 2.0 mm (min 1.6 mm). Ribs 2.0 mm. Internal cradles carry servo
  loads — the outer shell is a cover (xgoduck principle).
- 0.3 mm print clearance on mating faces. Moving-shell gap to neighbours:
  1.0 mm through the full range of motion (verified by sweep_check).

## Details
- Panel/parting lines: deliberate seams only, 0.4 mm wide × 0.3 mm deep groove.
- No random cutouts. Openings only if functional and designed: cable slot
  (rounded, 2 mm fillet), vent grille (Ø1.8 mm holes at 3.2 mm pitch, one
  consistent pattern), screw access, joint passages. Round every opening.
- Hardware hidden: servos, horns, cables and bearings sit inside shells or
  behind cover plates; the parting line is placed on purpose.

## Printability
- Every part prints without supports in a stated orientation
  (see docs/print_list.md), within the 220×220×250 bed.
- Generated by deterministic scripts in tools/ (build123d solids exported at
  0.05 mm / 0.1 rad, trimesh+manifold booleans for interface preservation).
  No hand-edited STL.
